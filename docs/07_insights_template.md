# Key insights (Day 7)

Write these only **after** you've built the dashboard and looked at the real numbers.
Never write an insight you can't point to on a chart. Each insight follows the same
pattern: **what you found → the number → why it matters → what to do.**

## Insight 1: Revenue trend (Q1)
- **Finding:** [e.g. revenue grew strongly between the two full years]
- **Evidence:** [Revenue YoY % = __ for __ vs __; peak month __]
- **So what:** [what this means for the business]
- **Recommendation:** [one action]

## Insight 2: Category concentration (Q2)
- **Finding:** [e.g. a small number of categories drive most revenue]
- **Evidence:** [top __ categories = __% of revenue (Pareto chart); bottom 10 = __%]
- **So what:** [dependency risk / where to focus stock and marketing]
- **Recommendation:** [one action]

## Insight 3: Where customers are (Q3)
- **Finding:** [e.g. revenue is heavily concentrated in one region]
- **Evidence:** [__ region = __% of revenue; __ state alone = __%]
- **So what:** [logistics, marketing reach]
- **Recommendation:** [one action]
