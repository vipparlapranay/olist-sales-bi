Olist Marketplace Analytics

99,441 orders · 74 categories · 94,983 customers · Jan 2017 – Aug 2018

Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (CC BY-NC 4.0), 99,441 orders placed 2016–2018. Figures produced by a Python pipeline — 22 data-quality checks, a two-fact star schema, ABC and RFM segmentation, a Mann–Whitney test and a late-delivery classifier — and reconciled line by line against the Power BI model. Revenue excludes cancelled and unavailable orders and is stated in Brazilian reais, not inflation-adjusted.
